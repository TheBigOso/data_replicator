ROW ACTIONS
Three verbs, three meanings
Suspend — graceful. Finishes the current cycle, drain progress visible, checkpoint held. Resume redoes at most one cycle, never loses correctness.
Disable — the administrative lock that survives colleagues. An ordinary resume refuses; an explicit re-enable is required.
Open event / Open window — event-driven work is not suspended here. It is cancelled from Events, which drains the job.
timeline → — every row lands on the stream timeline at its own timestamp. This is the reconciliation link.
BRANCH A · BULK ACTIONS
Never a count without a blast radius
"Suspend 6 jobs" is meaningless. The bar states which streams stop moving before the operator commits, and names how many of the selection are not directly controllable.

One selection model over every row — seeded, derived, and event-driven alike — so the checkbox behaves identically regardless of which builder produced the row. Bulk suspend is graceful per job, not a kill, and writes one ledger event naming every job and stream affected.
BRANCH B · THE QUEUE
WAITING is waiting on something
A bare WAITING chip is the least useful state in any scheduler. Each waiting job names its reason and, where it is queued behind others, its position.
Reasons: schedule (next fire time) · scheduler slot (N of M in use) · lock held by another job · a dependency job that has not finished.
Slot exhaustion is the one worth surfacing hardest: four refreshes queued behind four running ones is a capacity problem, not a fault, and the console should say so rather than let it read as a stall.
BRANCH C · WHAT EVENTS GAINS
Make it the evidence layer, not a feed
Filter by artifact type — plan diff · compare report · run log · export · enrollment token. Auditors search by artifact, not by time.
Stated retention window — a ledger with no retention policy is not an audit ledger. Say the window, and what happens at its edge.
Signed export — a range export the auditor can verify off-platform. Real gap for regulated buyers.
Diff view on definition changes — ACTIVATE and config-save events open the actual before/after, not a summary line.
BRANCH D · THE TIMELINE IS THE JOIN
Where the two tenses reconcile
Jobs and Events both link into the timeline at the right timestamp, so "what is running" and "what happened" can be read against each other on one axis.
A retrying capture, the activation two days before it, and the compare that failed after it are three rows on three screens — and one story on the timeline.
Sibling timelines at stream, VF, fleet, and global level all take the same links, so the join works at every scope.
JOB KINDS AND THEIR SHAPES
CAPTURE
cyclic · continuous
Reads the source log forever. One per activated stream. Its absence is the MISSING case.
INTEGRATE
cyclic · continuous
Applies the file log on its cadence. One per activated stream.
COMPARE
acyclic · scheduled or event-driven
Runs to completion. Event-driven ones are held by their event.
REFRESH
acyclic · scheduled or event-driven
Runs to completion, segmented and resumable. Cancel keeps loaded segments.
Cyclic vs acyclic is the useful split, not scheduled vs manual: a cyclic job is judged by whether it is keeping up, an acyclic one by whether it will finish. The two need different progress signals, and the list gives each the one that applies.
RULES THIS SCREEN HOLDS TO
· A job and its event are one record; a finished job becomes an event.
· Absence is a red row — the list reconciles against what is owed.
· No state chip stands alone; each says where it is in its curve.
· Suspend is graceful and resumable; disable survives colleagues.
· Bulk actions state their blast radius before they run.
· Event-driven work is cancelled from Events, never killed here.
· Every state change is ledgered with previous → new state.
